[i]Native English Description[/i]
[h1]Overview:[/h1]
Allows you to rename any country (even yourself).

[h1]Features:[/h1]
[olist]
 [*][b]Allow you to rename any country via the right-click context menu.[/b]
[/olist]

[h1]Note:[/h1]
[olist]
 [*] You may have to wait a couple of months to see updated names reflected on the map. Saving and reloading may speed this up.
